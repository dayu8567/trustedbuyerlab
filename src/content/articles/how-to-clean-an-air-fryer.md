Food residue becomes harder to remove after it cools and dries. A short cleaning routine after each use is easier on the appliance and on the person doing the washing. The manufacturer's manual remains the authority because materials and removable parts vary.

## Let it cool, then remove the basket

Unplug the appliance and let it cool according to the manual. Do not plunge a hot basket into cold water. Sudden temperature change can stress coatings and materials. Once the basket and tray are safe to handle, separate the removable parts.

Empty loose crumbs into the bin rather than washing a full load of grease down the drain. Check the manual before putting any part in a dishwasher; "dishwasher-safe" may apply only to some pieces.

## Use the gentlest cleaning method first

Warm water, a soft sponge, and a small amount of dish soap are usually enough when residue is fresh. Avoid abrasive pads, steel wool, and sharp tools. Those can scratch nonstick surfaces and make future sticking worse.

For baked-on grease, follow the manual's soaking guidance. A short soak can loosen residue without aggressive scrubbing. Do not use oven cleaner unless the manufacturer explicitly says it is compatible.

## Clean the heating area safely

The heating element and the area above the basket can collect splatter. After unplugging and cooling, use a damp cloth or soft brush only as directed. Do not spray water into the appliance body or immerse it. If residue is difficult to reach, check the manual rather than forcing access.

Inspect the vents and cord for grease or damage. A clean vent path helps airflow, while a damaged cord or plug should stop use until the issue is resolved.

## Dry everything before storage

Let removable parts dry completely before reassembling. Moisture trapped in a closed appliance can encourage odors. Wipe the exterior with a damp cloth and dry it immediately. Keep the cord loose rather than wrapping it tightly around a hot or sharp edge.

Store the fryer with enough clearance for the vents and door. If it lives under a cabinet, make sure the manual's clearance guidance still applies after cleaning.

## A two-minute habit

Empty the basket after every use, wash the removable parts when they are cool enough, and leave the appliance open to dry. A deeper cleaning can follow on a schedule that matches how often you cook greasy food.

The aim is not a spotless showroom finish. The aim is to keep airflow, removable parts, and food-contact surfaces in safe working condition.
